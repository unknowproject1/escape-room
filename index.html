WELCOME

Hello to everyone who has discovered this website.

This year, I moved into a new house. I thought this house was nice and that there was nothing strange about it.

Except that I found a basement a while ago.

Inside, I found a box full of VHS tapes. They seemed to be recordings of some kind of show.

I haven't watched the tapes yet, but I thought I should talk about this with someone.

I hope you spread the word about this blog.

Recovered VHS recording. The contents of this tape are currently being reviewed.

WHAT I FOUND

✦ ───────── ✦

After watching the recording, I decided to take a closer look at the box I found in the basement.

As I mentioned before, there were several VHS tapes inside. However, the tapes weren't the only things I found.

There were also small designs and sketches of the people involved in the show, along with various promotional materials.

I don't know how old any of these things are, or why they were kept here.

I'm going to show you everything I find in the following sections of this website, little by little.

From what I've been able to gather so far, it seems that this house may have belonged to the producer of the show.

I'm not completely sure about that yet.
